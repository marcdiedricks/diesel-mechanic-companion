## Control rule

External visual resources are optional online learning support only.

- The written KM lesson remains complete offline.
- External resources are never required for progress, evidence or assessment.
- External resources do not authorise practical work.
- No operational repair, pressure-release, lifting, live-testing, refrigerant, brake-release, high-pressure fuel or return-to-service tutorial is approved for learner-facing use.
- Links are opened only when the device is online.
- External resources are not cached by the PWA.
- Every resource must be rechecked if its source, content or availability changes.

## High-risk visual rule

Where a topic could become practical operating instruction, the app must keep the written conceptual lesson and safety boundary but must not add a learner-facing external tutorial merely to satisfy a visual quota.

This applies especially to:
- high-pressure fuel systems;
- brake release, adjustment or repair;
- hydraulic or pneumatic pressure release;
- vehicle lifting or supporting;
- electrical live testing;
- refrigerant handling;
- engine or heavy-component removal;
- return-to-service decisions.

## Offline rule

Loss of internet must never block:
- opening the PWA;
- reading KM lessons;
- using PM preparation support;
- using WM evidence support;
- recording local progress.

Visual learning remains supplementary.
