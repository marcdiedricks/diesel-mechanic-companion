## 4. SAFETY / AGE-APPROPRIATE BUILD BOUNDARY

This PWA is a learning companion, not a workshop operating manual.

Learner-facing content may cover:
- terminology and component recognition;
- system purpose and high-level theory;
- drawing/schematic interpretation;
- hazard recognition;
- diagnostic reasoning at a conceptual level;
- safe stop/escalate decisions;
- documentation, quality and evidence preparation;
- classroom calculations that do not enable hazardous physical activity.

Learner-facing content must not provide:
- step-by-step vehicle repair procedures;
- high-pressure fuel-system procedures;
- brake release/caging procedures;
- hydraulic lifting or load-handling procedures;
- electrical isolation or live testing procedures;
- refrigerant handling procedures;
- engine removal/installation procedures;
- machine/tool operating instructions;
- settings, sequences or techniques that enable unsupervised hazardous work.

Practical and workplace modules are represented as preparation/evidence support only and require competent adult supervision and approved provider/workplace processes.
